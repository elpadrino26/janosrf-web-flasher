JanOS SubGHz - Community Fork of ProjectZero

Sub-GHz done right for people who actually use the hardware — rolling gates, remotes, and a serious decoder lineup behind one UART-friendly stack.

Hardware lock-in (by design). This stack is built and tested for the original Lab5 C5Monster board only — the one with the CC1101 wired the way Lab5 intended. Clones and random breakouts are on their own.

Grab the janos_subghz_lib ESP-IDF components, wire JanOS, and you get RX/TX workflows, a manufacturer key table, and a console you can drive from a terminal or your favourite LVGL rig.

Flashing the firmware (replace XYZ with your port name): 
esptool --chip esp32c5 \
  -p /dev/tty.usbserial-XYZ \
  -b 460800 \
  --before=default-reset \
  --after=hard-reset \
  write-flash \
  --flash-mode dio \
  --flash-freq 80m \
  --flash-size 8MB \
  0x2000 bootloader.bin \
  0x10000 partition-table.bin \
  0x20000 projectZero.bin
Download the firmware (Mega.nz).

Below is what the firmware actually advertises today: 72 protocol families in the on-device registry, plus 105 named manufacturer / MF key slots in the embedded keystore (plus extra raw crypto blobs for a few rolling schemes). If it is not on these lists, do not expect magic.

NFC support includes reading and UID-only emulation for Ultralight and MIFARE DESFire cards. Card contents and DESFire applications are not read or emulated.
